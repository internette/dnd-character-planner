Lead with what a pick does at the table, then why it fits the character.

<DoDont
  do={<p>Hits up to five foes at once. Reflavor the weapon flourish as a gust of pistol shots.</p>}

## Use game units as written

Write units the way the books do: 20 ft, 1,500 gp, d10.

<DoDont do={<p>A 20 ft psychic burst.</p>} dont={<p>A 20-foot (6 m) psychic burst.</p>} />

## Sentence case, except labels

Titles, buttons and headings use sentence case. Labels (eyebrows, tags, column heads) are uppercase, set with `text-transform` in CSS so screen readers read words.

<DoDont do={<p>Before you level up</p>} dont={<p>Before You Level Up</p>} />

## The player decides

Mark a suggestion *Recommended* and put its alternatives directly under it. Don't phrase picks as requirements, and don't lecture about a choice the player has made.

<DoDont
  do={<p>Recommended: Hypnotic Pattern. Alternatives: Fear, for a cone that sends foes running.</p>}
  dont={<p>You must take Hypnotic Pattern.</p>}
/>

## Name the source

Every spell, feat or feature names its source book and level in its meta line, like `XGtE · 5th · Druid · Concentration`. Don't paste rules text from non-SRD books; describe what a pick does in your own short words.
