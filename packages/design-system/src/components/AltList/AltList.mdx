## Joiners

The name and reason are joined with ": " by default. When the reason reads on from the name, pass a different `joiner`, such as ", ".

<Canvas of={Stories.CustomJoiner} />

## Content rules

- List at most three alternatives.
- Each reason says what makes it a different choice, not why it is worse than the pick.

## Accessibility

The alternatives are a list, so screen readers announce how many there are. The chevron is decorative and hidden from assistive tech. Long reasons wrap under the text, not under the chevron.
