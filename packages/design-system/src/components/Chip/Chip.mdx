## Tones

| Tone | Use |
| --- | --- |
| `neutral` | Features gained, such as 5th-level spells or Song of Rest d8 |
| `instant` | Casting properties: Instant, No concentration |
| `concentration` | Casting property: Concentration |
| `dm` | Table-dependent content, such as non-core sources or Ask your DM |

## Content rules

- Use `dm` whenever the group must approve something. The table has the final word (constitution Part I §3).
- Write children in sentence case. The `instant`, `concentration` and `dm` tones are uppercased by CSS.

## Accessibility

The text is the content. Chips aren't interactive.
