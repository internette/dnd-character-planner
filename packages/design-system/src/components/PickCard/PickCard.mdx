## Labels

The label is "Recommended". When a level grants more than one pick, number them: "Recommended · Secret 1".

<Canvas of={Stories.SecretLabel} />

## Content rules

- The meta line always names the source book, then spell level and class list, such as "PHB · 3rd · Bard · Concentration".
- The description says what the pick does at the table, then why it fits the character, in one or two sentences of your own. Never paste text from the book.

## Accessibility

The name is an `h4`, because PickCard sits under a level card's `h3`. The gold label passes 4.5:1 on its tint in both themes.
