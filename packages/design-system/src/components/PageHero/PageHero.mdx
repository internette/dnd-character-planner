## Content rules

- One `xl` hero per page. Supplements use `lg`.
- The eyebrow names the edition and kind of page.
- The title is the character's name, never a slogan.
- The subtitle is one italic line: subclass, purpose, level range.
- Two or three uppercase identity tags.
- The swirl is decoration for Aero-themed characters; turn it off otherwise.

## Accessibility

`xl` renders `h1` and `lg` renders `h2`. Tags are a list. The swirl is hidden from assistive technology.

## Phones

Below 480px wide the `xl` title steps down to `display-lg` and the padding to `space-6`, so "Ciaccona" fits on a 375px phone.
